Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses your Google account to:

  • create one calendar named "משימות" in your Google Calendar, and read and write events in it;
  • read the list of your calendars;
  • read events in your calendars, to show them next to your tasks.

Where your data goes

Your tasks are stored on your phone and in the "משימות" calendar in your own Google account. The app has no server. The developer does not receive, collect, store or share any of your data, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

The app talks only to Google's own services, directly from your phone.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your phone. The "משימות" calendar stays in your Google Calendar until you delete it there.
